Bahcesehir University was founded in 1998 in Istanbul, Turkey.
It has over 20,000 students enrolled across various faculties.
The university offers programs in engineering, law, medicine, and arts.
Tuition fees vary by department and are listed on the official website.
The campus is located in Besiktas, one of Istanbul's central districts.
Student clubs include robotics, music, photography, and entrepreneurship.
